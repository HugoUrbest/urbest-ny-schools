How Urbest works

Three jobs your team does every day. One place to do them.

Work orders

Reported from a phone. Tracked to the finish.

Staff scan the QR code in the room or open the app, add a photo and send. The request lands on your team's board with its building and floor. You assign it, everyone sees where it stands, and the person who reported it hears back when it's done.

Building checks

Inspections that end in a dated report.

Build each checklist once and schedule it to come back when it's due. Mark every item green, orange or red, add a photo or a comment, then share the PDF report with the people concerned.

Recurring checks for New York public schools, for example

  1. Every year

    Fire safety inspection

    In each school building, before the State renews its certificate of occupancy.

    8 NYCRR §155.8 (opens in a new tab)

  2. Every six months

    Asbestos surveillance

    In buildings with asbestos-containing material, recorded in your AHERA management plan.

    40 CFR §763.92 (opens in a new tab)

  3. Every three years

    Lead in drinking water

    Outlets above 5 ppb stay out of service until they are fixed and retested.

    NYS Public Health Law §1110 (opens in a new tab)

  4. Every six months

    School bus inspection

    The State Department of Transportation inspects each bus. A bus that fails stays off the road until it passes.

    NYSDOT inspection (NYC Public Schools) (opens in a new tab)

Examples, not legal advice: your district's health and safety plan sets the full list. Sources checked on October 19, 2026.

FAQ

Questions from facilities directors

What is Urbest?

Urbest is a work order and maintenance platform (a CMMS) for organizations with many buildings. School districts use it to collect repair requests, run recurring building checks, maintain equipment and school buses, manage contractors and report costs.

Do teachers and staff need training?

No. People who report a repair use it right away, from the app, the web or a QR code. For your technicians and supervisors, setting up their access and starting to handle requests takes 5 to 30 minutes.

Can contractors use Urbest?

Yes. Each contractor gets their own access, limited to their sites and service categories. Quotes, completed work and payments go through your approval.

How is Urbest priced?

$3 per student, per year, with unlimited users: a school of 1,000 students costs $3,000 a year. You get a written quote before anything starts, and the demo includes a free 30-day trial.

Do we need to go out to bid?

It depends on the amount. New York's General Municipal Law §103 requires competitive bidding for purchase contracts over $20,000, counting what the district expects to spend over twelve months. At $3 per student, that threshold is about 6,600 students a year. Below it, your district's own purchasing policy applies.

Source: NYS Comptroller: Seeking Competition in Procurement (opens in a new tab)

Is Urbest present in the United States?

Yes. Urbest has a presence in New York, alongside its teams in Paris and London, and support is available in English.

Where is our data hosted? Can staff use single sign-on?

Urbest is hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS). Single sign-on (SSO) is available.

Who uses Urbest today?

More than 300 schools, including the public high schools of three French regions (Normandy, Centre-Val de Loire and Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes), and public administrations such as the Préfecture des Hauts-de-Seine. Urbest is rated 4.8 out of 5 on Capterra.
